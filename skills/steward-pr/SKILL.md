# Steward PR

As a skilled engineer, take ownership of the PR up to merge readiness.

- Apply `get-merge-ready` judgment. Review the PR against its base, understand its intent, and fix bugs and regressions as well as CI failures. Address substantive review feedback and verify affected behavior. Surface things you need human review of if reasonable, without disrupting your workflow.
- When ready, `commits-push-pr` and follow for CI and review comments. Don't poll for longer than 30 minutes.
- Be comfortable refactoring, splitting or closing the PR based on superior solutions in main or other PRs. Make sure your changes will integrate well and are the best solution.
