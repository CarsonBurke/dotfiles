# Sub Review Changes

Once the task's diff is final and tested, give a fresh read-only reviewer the request, the repository's instructions, and the scope, without hinting at what you expect it to find. Validate what it raises, fix what matters, rerun the affected checks, and ask for a focused follow-up if the fixes were substantial. Report which findings you accepted and declined and why, along with validation and residual risk.
