---
name: writing-emails
description: Use when writing an email
---

- Use the `email` CLI.
- Think about what the email is for, and imagine what the follow-ups will look like.
- Resist lists. They're sometimes right, but often a sign of being overly specific; not everything needs solving in the first email.
- If you're unsure whether an email is impactful, check with the user before sending.
