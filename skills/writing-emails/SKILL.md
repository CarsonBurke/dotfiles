---
name: writing-emails
description: Use when writing an email
---

- Use `email` cli.
- Think about what the purpose of the email is, and imagine what follow ups will look like.
- Avoid any signs of AI writing.
- Resist the desire to use lists. Sometimes they are reasonable, but often they are a sign of overspecificity. Not everything needs to be solved in the initial email.
- If you're unsure of it's an impactful email, you may want to surface to the user before sending.
